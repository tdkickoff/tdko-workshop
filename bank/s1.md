Reveal lines:

- **No. 15, Ruud Gullit:** Dreadlocked captain of the Netherlands' Euro 88 winners who later became the first overseas manager to win the FA Cup.
- **No. 16, Peter Schmeichel:** Captained Manchester United to the Champions League in his final game for the club, completing the 1999 Treble.
- **No. 17, Gianfranco Zola:** Scored the Cup Winners' Cup final winner seconds after coming on as a substitute, and was later voted Chelsea's greatest ever player.
- **No. 18, Dennis Bergkamp:** Arsenal's 'non-flying Dutchman', remembered for his touch, his vision and a famous spin-and-finish goal at Newcastle in 2002.
- **No. 19, Ian Rush:** Liverpool's all-time record goalscorer, with 346 goals across two spells at the club.
- **No. 20, Fernando Torres:** Scored the winner in the Euro 2008 final and once cost Chelsea a then-British record fee of £50 million.
- **No. 21, Patrick Vieira:** Born in Senegal, he became the driving force of Arsène Wenger's Arsenal and won the World Cup and the European Championship with France.
