Getting started

What is Multiplayer?

A live football quiz for up to 6 players, each on their own phone. One person hosts, shares a four-letter room code, and everyone plays the same questions at the same time. It's free and there's no sign-up.

How do I host a quiz?

Go to Multiplayer, tap Host a quiz, share the room code, pick your games and lengths, then tap Kick off once at least one friend has joined. There's a step-by-step hosting guide too.

How many quizzes can I host?

Up to 3 a day from each phone or computer. The limit resets each day. Joining other people's quizzes has no limit.

How do my friends join?

They open the invite link you share, or go to Multiplayer, tap Join a quiz and type your room code. Up to 6 players can be in a room, including the host.

Can we play on a TV?

Yes. Open tdko.app/tv in the TV's web browser (or cast a laptop) and type the room code. The TV shows the questions, the clock and the leaderboard; everyone still answers on their own phone.

Can we play in teams?

Yes. The host can choose everyone for themselves, 2 teams or 3 teams, and shuffle the teams. A team's score is its players' points added together.

How are typed answers judged?
  • Capitals, accents, spaces and symbols don't matter.
  • A surname is enough. A first name on its own never counts.
  • Who's That Baller needs the spelling right. Clean Sweep allows a small slip: one wrong letter for names of 5 to 8 letters, two for 9 or more.
  • In Clean Sweep, a surname shared by two players on the list gets you both.
What happens between rounds?

Everyone sees the scores and what's coming next, then taps I'm ready. When all players in the room are ready, a 5-second countdown starts the next round. The host can also start it straight away.

What happens if it's a tie?

If two or more players (or teams) finish level on points for first place, there's a tie-breaker. The tied players tap I'm ready, a wheel spins to pick one of the games you played, and after a 5-second countdown it's sudden death: the first to win a round outright wins the quiz. Everyone else watches. Ties for any other place share the position and the medal. And if nobody scored at all, the quiz simply ends.

What can the host do during a quiz?

Pause, skip the current question or round, start the next round early, or end the quiz. If the host's phone drops out for a while, hosting passes to another player in the room.

What if I lose signal or close the page?

On the same phone, open the invite link again or rejoin with the room code and you'll be back in, with your points. While you're away you can't score, and the quiz doesn't wait for you.

Fair play and privacy

What if an answer is wrong?

Every question is checked against a published source before it's used. If you spot a mistake, use Contact us or the "Report a problem" link. We check it, correct it, and fix anywhere else the same fact is used.

What do you keep about our quiz?

Only what the quiz needs while it's running: the room code, the names you pick and the scores. Once the room expires, nothing from it is kept. We count how many quizzes are played and finished, without any names. See our privacy information.

Is it free?

Yes. Multiplayer and the daily puzzles are free to play.
